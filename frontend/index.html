One clear record for every milk deal

AmataShield helps farmers, buyers, drivers and cooperatives in Rwanda agree on litres, price, lactometer evidence, delivery and payment, so everyone can point to the same time-stamped record.

How a deal works

1
Post or find milk. Farmers post litres and price. Buyers filter by price, litres and distance.
2
Book and confirm. The other party confirms within a short time, or the booking expires and you can try again. Both acceptances are time-stamped.
3
Hire a driver (optional). Riders and drivers apply with their wage. You choose one and they accept.
4
Lactometer check at pickup. Photograph the scale and enter the reading. Delivery is allowed when it fits the range the two parties agreed.
5
Buyer checks on arrival. The buyer repeats the check, then accepts or rejects.
6
Payment is recorded. The payer reports, the receiver confirms. If rejected, both sides are flagged until settled.

Built for everyone in the chain

Farmers

Post milk, see who accepted, keep proof of every delivery.

Buyers

Find milk near you, review evidence, accept or dispute.

Drivers and riders

Find delivery jobs and agree your wage up front.

Cooperatives

See deals of your members and help settle disputes.

What AmataShield does not do

It is a record, not a guarantee. AmataShield does not hold or send money, is not a bank or escrow service, and does not certify that milk is safe. A photo cannot prove milk quality. Lactometer readings are compared only with the range you and your partner agreed. AI features only suggest values; a person must confirm them.
